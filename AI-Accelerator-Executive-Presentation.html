01 The Three Core Operational Tracks

Rather than building generic compliance checklists, our AI platform provides three actionable, highly differentiated capabilities for businesses:

1. Code-to-GDPR Generator Static AI

"Give us your code, and we'll tell you what GDPR requirements you need to comply with."

Analyzes the entire GitHub repository, detects what data the application collects (PII, health, credentials), and generates tailored UK GDPR & India DPDPA statutory rules.

  • AST & Schema scanning (routes, ORM models, APIs).
  • Identifies Article 9 Special Category data.
  • Automates developer compliance action lists.

2. Document Review & Fix Document AI

"Give us your existing GDPR docs, and we'll find gaps & help correct them."

Uploads existing policies, vendor DPAs, or DPIA drafts. The engine spots legal omissions, flags ICO/DPDP fines risk, and auto-generates a corrected file.

  • Catches missing Article 9 explicit consent.
  • Corrects vague data retention ("as long as necessary").
  • Outputs clean, submission-ready Word/PDF files.

3. VM Sandbox Monitor Runtime Security

"Run the app in a VM sandbox to detect suspicious activity & data theft."

Observes runtime behavior in a controlled sandbox (file reads, DB extraction, network sockets). Flags bulk customer data theft & unauthorized exfiltration.

  • Real-time eBPF socket & process tracing.
  • Detects abnormal bulk database dumps.
  • Automated firewall kill-switch for exfiltration attempts.

03 Operational Workflow & System Architecture

How the application works in practice: an automated, continuous three-stage pipeline that ingests data, evaluates statutory compliance, and takes automated remediation actions.

STAGE 1: INGESTION
Product: Scans GitHub code, APIs, schemas & draft policies.

VDI: Monitors Citrix/AVD session clipboard & file sync events.
→
STAGE 2: AI REASONING
Dual-Law Evaluation:
• India DPDP Act 2023 Rules
• UK GDPR & ICO Guidelines
• PII Extraction (Aadhaar, PAN, NI)
• Article 9 Special Category Check
→
STAGE 3: REMEDIATION
Immediate Action:
• Auto-blocks remote clipboard leaks.
• Generates auto-fixed policy drafts.
• Generates living RoPA & DPIA files.

1. The Product & Document Workflow

When a developer or legal officer submits a draft policy, API schema, or tech stack, the AI detects missing legal terms (e.g. unstated retention periods, missing DPO contact), automatically rewrites the clauses, and produces a submission-ready PDF/Word document.

2. The VDI Runtime Protection Workflow

During remote virtual desktop sessions, if an operator or developer attempts to copy sensitive records across the virtual perimeter onto a local machine, the listener intercepts the clipboard event, redacts the PII, and logs an immutable audit event.
