Before We Start • Plain-English Meanings

4 Words You Will Hear (Explained Simply)

You do not need any technical background to use this platform. Here is what four common words mean in everyday legal terms:

📁 What is "GitHub"?

Think of GitHub like Google Drive or a locked digital filing cabinet where software teams store the written instructions (source code) of their website or mobile app. Instead of asking engineers 50 questions, you just share that folder's web link.

📐 What is "Source Code"?

Just like an architect's blueprint for a building, source code is the written instruction manual that tells an app what customer details to ask for (name, phone, date of birth) and where to store them.

🤝 What is a "Subprocessor"?

Simply an outside vendor or service provider that the client's app relies on — for example, a payment company (like Stripe or Razorpay), a cloud storage provider, or an AI tool. Under GDPR, you must list these vendors and sign data agreements with them.

📒 What are "RoPA" and "DPIA"?

RoPA (GDPR Article 30) is the official Record of Processing Activities logbook regulators ask to see. DPIA (GDPR Article 35) is a Data Protection Impact Assessment — a formal risk report required when handling sensitive data like health or children's info.

✅ Examples of Illegal Wording It Automatically Catches & Repairs:

  • Vague Storage Promises: Flags illegal lines like "we keep your data for as long as necessary" and inserts exact retention timelines (UK GDPR Art 5(1)(e) & DPDP Act Sec 8(7)).
  • Missing India Grievance Officer: Automatically writes and inserts the mandatory Grievance Redressal Officer contact block and 30-day resolution timeline (DPDP Act Sec 13).
  • Overseas Data Access: Adds UK ICO International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) protections whenever UK/EU data is accessed by teams in India or abroad.
  • Sensitive Data Consent: Inserts separate, unticked explicit consent wording whenever health or personal belief data is handled (UK GDPR Art 9).
