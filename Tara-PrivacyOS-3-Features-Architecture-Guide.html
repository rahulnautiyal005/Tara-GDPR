💡 Key Clarification: Does Feature 1 Generate ONLY a Privacy Policy or a FULL GDPR Package?

It generates a COMPLETE 5-Document GDPR & India DPDP Compliance Package — NOT just a Privacy Policy!
When you scan a GitHub repository, the UI opens the "Generated Privacy Policy" tab first by default, which can make it look like only a policy was created. Right next to that tab are 4 more mandatory statutory documents automatically generated from the exact same codebase scan:

1. Statutory Privacy Policy UK GDPR (Art 13/14) & India DPDP Act (Sec 5) notice tailored to your app's exact database fields.
2. Plain-English Summary Non-technical executive brief showing what data you collect, risks, and action items.
3. Article 30 RoPA Ledger Mandatory statutory Record of Processing Activities table required by ICO/EU regulators.
4. Risk Audit (DPIA) Article 35 Data Protection Impact Assessment flagging high-risk data (health, children, AI).
5. Developer Tech Patches Exact engineering checklist: consent checkboxes, DSAR delete endpoints, and AES-256 encryption.
Feature 1 • Code-to-Compliance

GitHub Repository to Full GDPR Compliance Package

Turns any public GitHub repository (Python, Node.js, TypeScript, Go, Java, C#, PHP, Rust) into a legally tailored 5-document UK GDPR & India DPDP Act compliance suite in seconds.

POST /api/github/extract-and-generate
💼 Non-Tech / Business Explanation

Think of it as an X-Ray Machine for Your App's Privacy

Normally, lawyers spend 2–3 weeks interviewing engineers to ask: "What user data do we store? Which third-party tools do we send it to?" Engineers often forget half of them.

  • Zero Manual Guesswork: You simply paste your GitHub repo link and company name.
  • Reads the Truth in Code: It inspects database tables (like email, dob, ip_address) and external tools (like Stripe, OpenAI, AWS, Razorpay).
  • Detects App Domain Automatically: Knows whether your repo is FinTech, HealthTech, Real Estate, Legal AI, or Social Media — so every policy is customized to your real product.
  • 5 Outputs Ready to Download: Privacy Policy, Executive Summary, Article 30 RoPA Ledger, Risk DPIA, and Developer Fix Tasks.
💻 Tech / Engineering Implementation

Fast Polyglot GitHub API Tree + AST/Schema Extraction

Instead of slow full-repo git clone operations, the scanner queries the GitHub Trees API directly in memory, filters high-signal files, and runs polyglot model/route parsers.

  • Fast Tree Extraction: Fetches only models, schemas, API routes, and package manifests (package.json, requirements.txt, go.mod, pom.xml, Cargo.toml).
  • Polyglot Parsers: Extracts fields from Python (SQLAlchemy, Pydantic, Django), JS/TS (Prisma, Mongoose, TypeORM, Zod), Go structs, Java JPA, C# EF Core, and PHP Laravel.
  • Case-Normalized PII Classifier: Converts firstName / dateOfBirth to canonical keys and separates Standard PII (Art 6) from Special Category Data (Art 9).
  • Subprocessor Registry: Maps 50+ vendor SDKs (OpenAI, Stripe, Twilio, AWS, PostHog) to Article 28 DPA & UK IDTA cross-border transfer mandates.

Feature 2 • Document Auto-Fixer

Existing Draft Document to Law-Compliant Document

Audits any uploaded or pasted Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, DPA, or Cookie Policy, identifies illegal or missing clauses, and automatically injects statutory legal fixes.

POST /api/rag/audit
💼 Non-Tech / Business Explanation

Like Grammarly, But for UK GDPR & India DPDP Law

Most startups copy-paste a generic privacy policy from the internet. Those drafts almost always contain illegal phrases like "We keep your data as long as necessary."

  • Spot Hidden Legal Traps: Paste your existing policy (or upload a file) and click Audit.
  • Clear Compliance Score: Gives an instant 0–100% score and flags Critical Flaws vs Warnings with exact fine exposure (up to £17.5M / ₹250 Cr).
  • Automatic Clause Injection: It doesn't just complain — it writes the exact legally compliant replacement paragraphs (e.g. India Grievance Officer, UK IDTA Transfer Clause, AI Zero-Retention Guarantee).
  • 1-Click Export: Download the upgraded document as Word (.docx), Text, or Copy to Clipboard.
💻 Tech / Engineering Implementation

AI JSON-Schema Auditor + Deterministic Statutory Fallback

Designed with a dual-tier execution pipeline so document auditing is both domain-intelligent and 100% fault-tolerant.

  • Primary AI Legal Audit: Dispatches document text + doc_type to Azure OpenAI (gpt-4o-mini) in strict response_format: json_object mode to evaluate against UK GDPR & DPDP Act 2023.
  • Structured Redline Schema: Returns normalized JSON containing stats (score, critical, warnings, fixes), per-issue citations, and injected fixed_text.
  • Deterministic Fallback Engine: If the external AI endpoint is offline, a rule-based statutory engine checks 6 core legal invariants (Art 9 explicit consent, Art 5(1)(e) vague retention, Chapter V IDTA, DPDP Sec 13 Grievance Officer, Sec 6(4) withdrawal, ICO AI Zero-Retention).
  • Document Assembly: Frontend renders side-by-side issue cards and the merged remediated document preview.

Feature 3 • Legal AI Copilot

Dual-Layer Conversational Legal AI Chatbot

A 24/7 Privacy Counsel & DPO combining a verified statutory knowledge base (RAG) with an autonomous LLM Arbiter, full multi-turn memory, and Hinglish/casual language fluency.

POST /api/chat
💼 Non-Tech / Business Explanation

A Smart Lawyer Who Remembers Your Context & Speaks Your Language

Unlike rigid old chatbots that repeat the same legal textbook template on every message, Tara AI talks like an experienced human DPO in a meeting.

  • Understands Casual & Hinglish Input: Handles "hlo", "kaise ho", or off-topic questions (like "pm of india?") gracefully without dumping fake legal memos.
  • Remembers Earlier Messages: If you say "My company is Law AI, do I need GDPR?" and then ask "what about in India?" or "what were my earlier questions?", it remembers seamlessly.
  • Answers Out-of-the-Box Questions: Even if a question is outside UK/India statutes (e.g., California CCPA or Schrems II), it uses its broader legal brain to answer accurately.
  • Zero Hallucinated Penalties: Strictly distinguishes GDPR Legitimate Interests (Art 6(1)(f)) from India's DPDP Act (Sec 7), and cites exact DPDP penalties (up to ₹250 Cr).
💻 Tech / Engineering Implementation

Single-Pass Dual-Layer RAG + LLM Arbitration (Zero Extra Latency)

Executes intent classification, in-memory hybrid retrieval, conversation memory injection, and LLM verification inside a single HTTP inference pass (~1s total).

  • Intent Router: classify_user_intent() filters greetings, Hinglish, meta-feedback, and trivia in <1ms before touching the RAG index or LLM.
  • Hybrid In-Memory Retriever: Combines BM25 + TF-IDF cosine similarity + exact statutory citation boosting over STATUTORY_CORPUS in ~3–5ms.
  • Dual-Layer LLM Arbiter: Tags retrieval confidence (HIGH vs PARTIAL/LOW). In one unified prompt, Azure OpenAI filters irrelevant chunks and bridges gaps via pre-trained legal knowledge.
  • Explicit Memory Injection: Passes last 8 turns as OpenAI chat messages plus an explicit CONVERSATION MEMORY summary block to guarantee 100% recall.
