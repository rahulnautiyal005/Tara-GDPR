## 1. What Is This Tool & How Does It Work?

Instead of paying £10k–£30k/year to bulky platforms like OneTrust or Sprinto, we are building a dedicated **AI Compliance Copilot (Document Auditor & Auto-Fixer)**:

```
[ Step 1: Input Draft ] ──────► [ Step 2: AI Audit ] ──────► [ Step 3: Auto-Fix & Output ]
• Messy Privacy Policy          • Scans against UK GDPR &       • Auto-rewrites flawed clauses
• Product Tech Stack / APIs       India DPDPA 2023              • Inserts statutory legal text
• Practitioner / Vendor DPA     • Detects red-flag risks        • Generates FRESH compliant file
• AI / LLM Data Processing        (Fines up to £17.5M)            ready to submit / download!
```

1. **Step 1: Upload / Input Draft Document:**
   - A business or developer inputs their messy draft (Draft Privacy Policy, App Architecture/Tech Stack, Practitioner Contract, or AI/OpenAI prompt workflow).
2. **Step 2: Instant AI Legal Audit:**
   - Cross-references the document against **UK GDPR (ICO rules)** and **India DPDPA 2023**.
   - Catches high-risk compliance flaws:
     * *Missing Article 9 Explicit Consent* (for sensitive consultation data).
     * *Vague Data Retention* ("retaining as long as necessary" is illegal under ICO rules).
     * *Unapproved India-to-UK Cross-Border Transfers* (missing IDTA / SCC contracts).
     * *Practitioner Data Leakage* (experts copying customer phone numbers to personal devices).
3. **Step 3: Auto-Fix & Fresh Compliant File Generation:**
   - AI doesn't just list errors — it **rewrites and injects compliant clauses** into the document.
   - Generates a fresh, submission-ready document (Final Privacy Notice, Article 30 RoPA, DPIA Assessment, or Vendor DPA) ready for download (.pdf / .docx) or publishing on the website/app.
