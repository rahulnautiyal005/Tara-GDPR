Feature 2 • Automated Redlining & Clause Repair

Turn Any Rough Legal Draft into a Compliant Document

When a client hands you an outdated or copy-pasted Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, or Vendor Agreement, this tool reviews every paragraph against UK GDPR and India's DPDP Act 2023 and writes the missing legal clauses for you.

✍️ Simple Legal Analogy:

Think of this as a senior privacy associate doing a first-pass redline review. It highlights dangerous or unlawful wording (like "we keep your data for as long as necessary"), explains which exact section of the law it breaks, and automatically inserts the legally compliant replacement wording.

How You Use It (3 Simple Steps):
1

Paste or Upload Draft

Choose the document type (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, DPA, or Cookie Policy) and paste or upload the client's existing draft.

2

View Legal Gaps & Score

See an instant compliance score (0–100%) along with a clear list of Critical Violations and Warnings citing exact Articles and Sections.

3

Export Upgraded Draft

Review the newly inserted statutory clauses side-by-side and download the corrected document as a Word file (.docx) or text.

✅ Examples of Legal Gaps It Automatically Catches & Fixes:

  • Vague Data Retention: Replaces illegal wording like "as long as necessary" with clear timeframes under UK GDPR Article 5(1)(e) and DPDP Act Section 8(7).
  • Missing India Grievance Officer: Automatically adds the mandatory Grievance Redressal Officer notice and 30-day resolution timeline under DPDP Act Section 13.
  • Cross-Border Transfers: Inserts UK ICO International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) safeguards when data is accessed by overseas teams.
  • Sensitive Data Consent: Flags missing unbundled explicit consent checkboxes under UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a).
